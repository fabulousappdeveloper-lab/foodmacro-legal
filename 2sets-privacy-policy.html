Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

This policy explains how Fabulous App Developer handles information in the 2Sets iPhone app. 2Sets helps you plan and record general strength-training workouts.

Information stored on your iPhone

Workout schedules and preferences, exercise weights and repetitions, workout history, notes you write, rest-alert settings, and optional progress photos are stored locally on your device. In this release, 2Sets does not send these records to a 2Sets server. There is no 2Sets account, cloud sync, advertising, analytics, or social community.

Camera and progress photos

If you choose to take a progress selfie, iOS asks for camera access. The photo is saved with your local workout recap and is not uploaded to us. Camera access is optional; you can use workout tracking without it. You can delete app data from Profile in the app. Device backups are governed by your Apple settings.

Notifications and audio

If enabled, local notifications can remind you when a rest period ends. Spoken exercise and rest cues play while the app is open. 2Sets does not access your microphone.

Music links

Music shortcuts open third-party music services you choose. Those services may collect information under their own privacy policies. 2Sets does not access your music account.

Children

2Sets is a general fitness utility and is not designed to collect information from children. The app has no 2Sets account or online community.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy if the app's features or data practices change. For privacy questions, email fabulousappdeveloper@gmail.com.
