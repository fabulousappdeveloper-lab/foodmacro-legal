FOODMACRO

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 18, 2026

Overview

FoodMacro helps people log meals, set nutrition goals, and receive AI-assisted estimates from meal photos. This policy explains what information FoodMacro handles, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information FoodMacro handles

  • Nutrition entries and goals. Meals, calorie and macro goals, favorites, and appearance preferences are stored locally on your device so the app can provide its tracking features.
  • Meal photos you choose to analyze. When you choose a photo and tap Analyze Meal, the photo is sent securely to FoodMacro's analysis service to generate estimated foods, calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fat.
  • Purchase status. Apple processes FoodMacro Premium purchases. FoodMacro receives subscription-entitlement information from the App Store to provide Premium access.

How meal-photo analysis works

FoodMacro sends the photo you select to a FoodMacro service hosted by Render. That service sends the image to OpenAI to generate the meal estimate. FoodMacro does not sell meal photos or use them for advertising. FoodMacro's analysis service is designed to process the photo for the requested analysis and does not intentionally store the submitted photo after processing.

Render and OpenAI process information on FoodMacro's behalf to provide this feature. Their handling of data is also governed by their respective privacy terms and policies.

How information is used

FoodMacro uses this information only to provide the app's nutrition tracking, meal logging, Premium access, and requested meal-photo analysis. FoodMacro does not use your information for targeted advertising.

Retention and deletion

Nutrition entries and preferences remain on your device until you remove them or delete the app. Meal photos are submitted only when you request analysis and are not intentionally retained by FoodMacro after processing. You may contact us to ask questions about or request deletion of information held by FoodMacro.

Your choices

Camera access is optional. You can decline or revoke camera permission in your device settings; the rest of FoodMacro remains available, although photo analysis will not work. You can remove local FoodMacro data by deleting the app from your device.

Children

FoodMacro is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when FoodMacro changes. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
